Geometry Relaxation: the Chemical-Shell Arm

Every result on this site so far used ideal defect geometries — the defect atom placed exactly at the substituted site, all neighbours frozen at lattice positions. This page adds the relaxation arm for all three MoS$_2$+SOC defects: constrained BFGS (defect + nearest Mo triangle + the full second-neighbour S cage; strictly fixed cell), a rerun of the full SOC SCF → $\Delta V$ → EDI → $H_{\rm eff}$/spectral chain at the relaxed geometries, and the supercell-DFT truth gate at both geometries. The findings split into a physics layer (O$_S$ changes qualitatively, V$_S$ and Se$_S$ barely move) and a methods layer (the truncated-Bloch $H_{\rm eff}$ degrades on distorted geometries — and would have reported a 0.22 eV phantom shift for V$_S$ had the truth gate not been standing).

1 · Setup: the chemical-shell constraint

The relaxation set is defined by neighbour shells of the defect site, not by a radius picked per defect. Around an S site the shells are: 3 Mo at 2.413 Å, then a near-degenerate S cage — the cross-layer S at 3.126 Å and six in-plane S at 3.185 Å, only 0.06 Å apart — and a clean 0.8 Å gap before the next shell. The chemical-shell convention frees the defect atom + Mo triangle + the whole S cage: 11 atoms for O$_S$/Se$_S$, 10 for V$_S$ (97 frozen via if_pos 0 0 0). The cell is strictly fixed — the supercell lattice must remain exactly 6× the primitive for the $\Delta V$ folding and FFT commensurability, so calculation='relax', never vc-relax.

Geometry is produced scalar-relativistically (SR SG15 v1.0 partners of the campaign's FR-1.0 pseudos, 100 Ry, $\Gamma$; forces are insensitive to SOC) and the electronic chain then reruns fully relativistically at the relaxed coordinates with the original campaign inputs — the only changed lines in the SOC SCF are the 108 coordinate rows. BFGS converges in 14–16 steps for all three. One honesty number: at convergence the largest force on a frozen atom is 0.008 Ry/au (the shell-4 Mo triangle) — that is the price of the truncation, identical across defects by construction.

The strongest sanity check is chemical: the relaxed bond lengths land on the natural values of the parent compounds — Mo–O at 2.07 Å and Mo–Se at 2.54 Å, neither of which was put in by hand. Oxygen sinks (small atom, short bond), selenium pops out (large atom), the vacancy's neighbours barely move. Note also what the table does not imply: relaxation energy is a poor predictor of level shifts, as §4 shows — the 0.118 eV V$_S$ relaxation moves its deep levels by 8 meV, while the methods-layer artifact it triggers is 30× larger.

3 · The rerun chain, and two operational notes

Per defect: relaxed-geometry SOC SCF (1.5 h, identical cost to ideal) → extract_pot → EDI direct $144\times144$ (v8, single node + OpenMP) → 21312/20160-dim $H_{\rm eff}$ diagonalization + full-window spectral function → supercell truth gate. Two notes worth recording:

  • Pipeline integrity gate for free: extract_pot writes the pristine potential alongside the defect one; since the pristine supercell is untouched by the defect relaxation, its cube must be byte-identical to the campaign original — and is (matching md5 for all three defects). A silent geometry or grid inconsistency anywhere upstream would break this.
  • The Banff single-node boundary, pinned: the SOC case needs a fixed $\sim$320 GiB of per-$k$ wavefunction cache regardless of layout, so a 375 GiB Banff node admits exactly one configuration — 4 MPI ranks × 56 OpenMP threads (NUMA-clean, 2 ranks/socket). Measured: MaxRSS 369.5/375 GiB (98.5%), 3136 s, no OOM; 6+ ranks provably cannot fit. The same run takes 1600–1750 s on one Anvil highmem node at 48×2 (more pools, better thread efficiency) — pools beat threads whenever memory allows.

5 · Methods layer: distorted geometries inflate the push-up — and the truth gate catches it

The relaxed-geometry $H_{\rm eff}$ (pristine Bloch basis + $\tilde\chi$, exactly the production method) was run in parallel with the truth. At ideal geometry its known systematic error is the +45 meV-class common mode. At relaxed geometry that error inflates:

The mechanism is the same one that runs through the augmentation page: the basis is the ideal-crystal Bloch set, and a distorted defect environment moves the true wavefunctions away from its span, so the Ritz values are pushed up. The ranking is exactly the vulnerability ranking established there — the vacancy is worst (its dangling-bond states have no augmentation orbital to adapt with; even 0.065 Å of neighbour motion costs +0.2 eV of representability), O$_S$ intermediate (its $\tilde\chi$ sits at the relaxed site, but the host bands don't know about the distortion), Se$_S$ immune (its states are host-like). Two practical consequences:

  • The standing rule did its job. "Supercell truth arbitrates all mid-gap claims" is what kept the +0.22 eV V$_S$ phantom out of the physics layer of this page. An $H_{\rm eff}$-only relaxation study would have published it.
  • Relaxed geometries need basis adaptation before the $T$-matrix numbers can be trusted quantitatively. Candidate cures, in the spirit of A1: displaced-atom $\tilde\chi$ for the shell atoms (Mo/S at their relaxed sites), or a vacancy-site $\tilde\chi$ built from the removed atom's orbitals — the same construction that was proposed for the PAW vacancy disease.

6 · Spectral functions, ideal vs relaxed

These maps inherit the $H_{\rm eff}$ vertex, so they carry its relaxed-geometry biases — the annotations state where each feature actually belongs per the truth gate. The qualitative content survives the caveat: O$_S$ gains an in-gap flat line, V$_S$ keeps one resonance and its SOC splitting, Se$_S$ changes nothing.

7 · A pure-displacement control probe: one S atom, +0.3 Å along $z$

Section 5's phantom conflates two things: the chemical defect (foreign species / vacancy) and the geometric distortion (host atoms off their pristine sites). This probe isolates the second. The perturbation is a pristine 6×6 supercell with a single S atom displaced $+0.3$ Å along $z$ (Mo–S bonds stretched $\sim$8%; between V$_S$'s 0.07 Å cage shifts and O$_S$'s 0.53 Å sink) — no species change, no vacancy, augmentation OFF by construction (there is nothing foreign to augment with; the displaced-S $\chi$ is precisely the missing object). The full chain ran on Banff: FR-SOC supercell SCF, $\Delta V$ extraction (pristine cube md5-identical to the campaign original), EDI direct with the displaced-S KB projectors carried automatically by the defect cube's atom list, $T$-matrix downfolding, spectral function, supercell truth gate.

What the probe establishes. (i) A displacement alone — zero chemistry — produces a genuine truncation error in the pristine-Bloch downfolding: the 140-band host space cannot cheaply rebuild wavefunction structure around an atom that is no longer where the basis expects it. (ii) The error is state-selective exactly as the augmentation-page law predicts: the occupied state carrying displaced-S $p$ character takes the +47 meV hit, the delocalized conduction-edge states take $\sim$5 meV. (iii) The magnitude ladder now has three measured rungs — one S × 0.3 Å → 47 meV; V$_S$ relaxed cage (11 atoms × ≤0.07 Å, dangling-bond state) → 216 meV; localization of the affected state, not displacement amplitude alone, sets the damage. (iv) It handed the displaced-$\chi$ implementation a clean calibration target — met the same day, below.

The cure, implemented and calibrated: displaced-atom $\chi$ (edi_v8a)

The augmentation machinery was center-agnostic all along — the site enters only as a continuous coordinate in a one-atom structure factor. What was missing was the ability to choose the site: the legacy input picks the first atom of a given species, which for a displacement defect is the wrong atom. A new input, aug_atom (an atom index into the defect cube's own list), selects the displaced atom directly; the species, the $\chi$ radials, and — the point — the displaced center then all come from the defect geometry. About 30 lines (edi_v8a.x; the legacy path is untouched). The host S pseudopotential carried no PSWFC, so scalar-limit 3$s$/3$p$ radials were grafted from the FR pseudo's own $(2j{+}1)$-averaged KB operators (the fravg construction of the augmentation page): $\varepsilon_{3s}=-1.268$, $\varepsilon_{3p}=-0.519$ Ry, eight spinor orbitals. The $(1-P)\chi$ Gram spectrum spans $5\times10^{-3}$–$5\times10^{-2}$ — the displaced $\chi$ overlaps the host span heavily, as expected, but all eight directions survive cleanly.

This validates the mechanism (the phantom is exactly the missing displaced-atom flexibility) and arms the machinery for its real target: multi-center augmentation (defect $\chi$ + displaced-neighbor $\chi$) against the relaxed-geometry phantoms of §5, V$_S$'s 0.22 eV foremost.

Multi-center $\chi$ (edi_v8b/c), calibrated on the relaxed-V$_S$ 0.22 eV phantom

The extension to many centers (aug_atoms, a list of defect-cube atom indices; Gram truncation via aug_keep; free-position ghost centers via aug_ghost_*) was calibrated against the hardest §5 case: the relaxed vacancy, whose $e$-pair shift phantom is +217 meV against a truth of +7.7. Verdict, in three layers. (i) The displacement-specific inflation — the 210 meV by which the relaxed no-aug overshoot exceeds the ideal one — is $\sim$83% removed: displaced-neighbor $\chi$ does what it was built for, and the ladder saturates by keep 60 (keep 76 adds 1 meV). (ii) The residual shift error ($\sim$+33 meV) traces to the vacancy-intrinsic deficiency present in both arms: the $a_1$ state, concentrated in the void, barely responds to neighbor $\chi$ (+133 → +124 meV overshoot). (iii) Two structural facts, learned the hard way and now load-bearing: Bloch-sum augmentation cannot distinguish centers that are primitive-lattice translates (the 144-k structure already supplies per-cell resolution — one center per inequivalent site; the ideal-geometry 10-center attempt is exactly singular), and by the same theorem, "$\chi$ at the vacancy site" is already implicit whenever any same-species center sits on that primitive site — the void state's stubbornness is not a coverage gap but a statement that it is not atomic-$\chi$-shaped. A more diffuse ghost (Se radials at the vacancy, aug_ghost_species) recovers 24 meV of the $a_1$ overshoot and 2 meV of the $e$-pair — diminishing returns that mark the practical boundary of A1-form augmentation. The supercell truth gate remains the standing rule for relaxed-geometry defect levels.

Ops footnote: the SCF also settled the hybrid-layout question — single-node 56 MPI × 4 OMP beats flat 224 MPI by 18% (1h13m vs 1h29m), while 2-node pw.x is 10–15× slower per iteration (Γ-point FFT all-to-all across the fabric) and is now banned for supercell SCF; EDI itself is fine on 2 nodes (k-pair pools): 737 s at 48 pools vs 3136 s at 4.
